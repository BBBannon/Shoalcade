How to install

  1. Scan the code with your phone's camera, or download the file and copy it to your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from this source.
  3. Open Shoalcade and log in with your Jellyfin address and your Jellyfin username and password.

Your Jellyfin address:

Your phone must be able to reach this address. If you opened this page as “localhost” or “127.0.0.1”, open it with your server's network address instead.
